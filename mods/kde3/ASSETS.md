# Historical KDE artwork

Unmodified HighColor PNGs from the KDE project archives, retained at their original 16/32px sizes. Copyright belongs to the original KDE contributors. Archive license: GPL v2 (`COPYING-KDE-ASSETS`). KDE and Konsole are KDE trademarks; this is a personal Herdr theme, not an official KDE product.

Sources (downloaded 2026-11-11; no KDE executables installed):
- https://download.kde.org/Attic/2.2.2/src/kdebase-2.2.2.tar.bz2
- https://download.kde.org/Attic/2.2.2/src/kdelibs-2.2.2.tar.bz2

These pre-Crystal icons match the early KDE era of the user-provided 2002 screenshot, including Konsole’s shell-in-monitor icon and the silver K/gear. The blue KDE terminal picture, B-II-style control vectors, LCD digits and menu banner are newly authored SVG recreations rather than extracted screenshot UI.

## Moheli window decoration
Adapted from Olivier Fourdan’s Moheli theme in official Xfce xfwm4:
https://github.com/xfce-mirror/xfwm4/tree/30eb3437bb9d410eeb17b22412c53505d26e3176/themes/moheli
GPL license, original source XPMs, themerc, palette and conversion manifest are
retained under `icons/moheli/`. The SVGs retain pixel geometry and resolve symbolic
GTK colors to classic blue active / gray inactive. No Xfce executable is installed.
